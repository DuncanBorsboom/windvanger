Frequently asked questions

Aren't vertical turbines noisy?

No. Our Savonius and helix rotors spin slowly (max ~300 rpm) and measure around 38 dB at 2 metres. For comparison: a library is ≈ 40 dB, an average heat pump 40–50 dB, a fridge 35–45 dB and a quiet house at night ≈ 30 dB. Our turbine sits at the same level as your fridge — quieter than the heat pump most people already have on their wall or in their garden.

What about net metering? Do I lose money feeding power back?

Good question — the Dutch net-metering scheme is being phased out by 2027 (66% in 2025, 33% in 2026, then 0%). Feed-in payments will therefore shrink. That's exactly why our philosophy revolves around self-consumption: wind generates at night and in winter, precisely when you use power or can fill your battery. Combine a turbine with a home battery and you sell almost nothing back — no dependence on what politicians decide about net metering.

Do I need a permit?

In most Dutch and Belgian municipalities our wall and roof models fall under the permit-free building track. For mast models we help you with the application.

Does this work with my existing solar panels and battery?

Yes. Every turbine ships with a hybrid inverter that connects in parallel with your solar setup. Already have a home battery (e.g. from your solar installer)? We link to it via the pairing kit.

What does such a turbine really generate per year?

At a typical Dutch site with decent wind (4–6 m/s average): the Breeze ± 400 kWh/year, the Stormbird ± 1,800 kWh/year. That's roughly 10% and 50% of an average household respectively.

What happens during a power outage?

The inverter's island mode takes over within 20 milliseconds. You won't notice — except that your neighbours are sitting in the dark.

Rules per municipality

Small wind turbines fall under the permit-free building track in most municipalities — but the details differ. Here's the current picture.

01

Wall & roof (up to 2 m)

Permit-free in almost all municipalities, as long as the turbine stays below the roofline. Always check the local zoning note first.

02

Garden mast (up to 4–5 m)

Often permit-free under the "limited permit-free" track, but some municipalities cap at 3 m or require distance to the property line. We handle the application with you.

03

Noise & safety

Our turbines stay under 40 dB — well within every municipal norm. Catch arms and brake systems prevent loose parts, even in storms.

04
